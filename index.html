Everyday actions that help

🪟

Protect indoor air

Close windows when outdoor pollution is high. Ventilate when outdoor air improves; use a correctly sized HEPA purifier if available.

Why it matters: limits outdoor particle entry. Results depend on room sealing, filters and airflow.
🚌

Choose a lower-emission trip

Use transit, share rides, combine errands and avoid idling. Choose a route away from busy roads when practical.

Why it matters: fewer private-vehicle trips reduce fuel use and local tailpipe emissions.
🍳

Reduce indoor combustion

Prefer electric cooking where possible, use an externally vented kitchen hood and avoid burning incense, candles or biomass indoors.

Why it matters: combustion can add particles and gases directly inside the home.
🧽

Clean without raising dust

Damp-wipe and mop rather than dry sweep. Use a vacuum with effective filtration if available.

Why it matters: damp cleaning helps stop settled dust from becoming airborne again.
😷

Be careful outdoors

When pollution is poor, reduce strenuous outdoor activity. A well-fitting certified particulate respirator can reduce particle exposure.

Why it matters: respirators filter particles, not all pollutant gases; fit is essential.
🌱

Act as a community

Share rides, avoid idling, support clean energy and report open waste burning through local channels.

Why it matters: collective choices can reduce emissions at source over time.
