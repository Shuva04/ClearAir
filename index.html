Recommendations that make a difference

🪟

Protect indoor air

Keep windows closed during pollution peaks. Ventilate when outdoor air improves; use a properly sized HEPA purifier if available.

Impact: reduces exposure to outdoor particles entering the room; purifier performance depends on room size and filter.
🚲

Move with less pollution

Walk, cycle or use transit on routes away from heavy traffic when conditions allow. Combine errands to avoid extra trips.

Impact: fewer car trips and less local tailpipe pollution; personal exposure varies by route and AQI.
🍳

Cook cleaner

Use electric cooking when possible, run exhaust ventilation, and avoid burning candles, incense or biomass indoors.

Impact: reduces combustion-related particles and gases generated inside your home.
🧽

Dust without stirring

Damp mop or wipe. Avoid dry sweeping and vacuuming without suitable filtration during high-pollution periods.

Impact: helps prevent settled particles from being re-suspended in indoor air.
😷

Use protection outdoors

When pollution is high, reduce exertion and time outside. A well-fitting certified particulate respirator may help protect against particles.

Impact: respirators reduce inhaled particles when fitted and worn correctly; they don't remove gases.
🌱

Community choices

Share rides, avoid vehicle idling, support clean energy and report open burning through local channels.

Impact: collective changes can lower emissions; effects depend on scale and local sources.
